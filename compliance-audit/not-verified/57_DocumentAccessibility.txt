--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY IT IS NOT VERIFIED:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The downloads and investors section of the website contain SEBI compliance filings, MOA/COI documents,
and newspaper publications. These PDFs are static images or scanned document copies. We cannot programmatically
verify document accessibility (OCR searchability, tagged headings, reading order metadata) purely from 
the front-end codebase repository.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST EVIDENCE REQUIRED TO MARK COMPLIANT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To achieve compliance, the user must run each PDF through Adobe Acrobat Pro Accessibility Checker 
or PAC (PDF Accessibility Checker) to ensure:
1. All scanned documents are OCR-enabled (searchable text).
2. The documents have proper tagged headings (H1, H2) and alternative texts on figures.
3. Language metadata is declared within the PDF.
